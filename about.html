About

Designer, editor, communicator.

The person behind Genesis Graphics.

Hello, I'm Igbinedion Nosakhare

I turn ideas into designs people remember.

I'm a graphic designer based in Benin City, Edo State, working under the name Genesis Graphics. I design flyers, posters, social media graphics, logos, event and campaign materials, and more. I also offer professional editing for written content.

I'm a Political Science student at the University of Benin, and I've served as Public Relations Officer I of the National Association of Political Science Students (NAPSS), UNIBEN Chapter. That role put me close to real publicity work: announcements, programmes and the graphics that carry them.

I've taught at Global Group of Schools and Key to Success Secondary and Primary School, and worked at Triumphant Computer Institute as both a computer teacher and a graphic designer. Teaching taught me to explain things simply, so I keep clients involved at every step.

I also help with editing and proofreading, including work on the autobiography The Path Beneath My Feet.

How I work

My design process

  1. Brief

    You tell me what you need, who it's for and where it will be used.

  2. Concept

    I develop a direction: layout, type, colour and imagery.

  3. Design

    I build the design and refine it with your feedback.

  4. Deliver

    You receive final files ready for social media, screen or print.
